Buyer Personas

Three archetypes that account for the majority of Nodestream pipeline. Use these to route discovery, tailor the pitch, and anticipate objections before they surface.

Persona 01 — Frontier Lab

The Frontier Lab

"We need raw throughput at the frontier of what's possible — and we needed it yesterday."

Who They Are
  • AI research orgs and well-funded startups training frontier models
  • Teams of 10–200 ML researchers and engineers
  • Burning through compute budgets of $5M–$50M+ per year
  • Decision makers: CTO, VP of Research, Head of Infrastructure
What They Need
  • Maximum GPU density (H100/B200 clusters, 256–2,048 GPUs)
  • Low-latency interconnects — InfiniBand HDR/NDR non-negotiable
  • Burst capacity with minimal lead time (<2 weeks ideal)
  • Dedicated bare-metal — no noisy-neighbor risk
  • Flexible term (12–36 mo) with renewal options
Pain Points
  • Cloud GPU queues and spot-instance interruptions killing run schedules
  • Hyperscaler pricing unpredictability — AWS/Azure bills ballooning
  • Compliance/data-sovereignty pressure from investors or gov contracts
  • Wasted researcher time waiting on infra instead of shipping
Buying Signals
  • Actively benchmarking providers or just came off a failed cloud run
  • Recent funding round (Series A–C) — budget is real and moving fast
  • Hiring infra engineers — scaling signal
  • Asking about InfiniBand topology or RDMA specifics early
Common Objections & Responses
"We're already on AWS Reserved."
Show 3-year TCO delta — bare-metal typically 40–60% cheaper at this scale. We'll model it together in the Configurator.
"What's your uptime SLA?"
99.9% hardware availability SLA with direct ops escalation — no ticket queue, dedicated POC.
"We need to move in 2 weeks."
That's exactly why we exist. Get hardware details from ops today, LOI this week, live next week.
30-Second Pitch
"You're burning researcher time and millions on cloud GPU queues that don't guarantee availability. Nodestream gives you dedicated bare-metal H100/B200 clusters with InfiniBand, no noisy neighbors, and a 2-week deployment window. You get the throughput, we handle the hardware — and on a 3-year basis you're likely cutting your compute bill in half."
Typical ACV$8M – $30M
Deal Length24 – 36 months
Sales Cycle4 – 10 weeks
Key ToolsDiscovery Scorecard (section 2) → Deal Configurator → Full Quote
Persona 02 — Neocloud

The Neocloud

"We resell GPU capacity to our customers — margins live and die by what we pay for hardware."

Who They Are
  • Infrastructure platforms reselling GPU compute as a service
  • Engineering-heavy teams of 20–500 people
  • Revenue-generating businesses with real customer demand today
  • Decision makers: CEO/Founder, VP Infrastructure, CFO
What They Need
  • Volume pricing and favorable long-term rate cards
  • Predictable cost basis to protect margin on customer contracts
  • Multi-cluster capacity with geographic flexibility
  • API-accessible infrastructure for orchestration integration
  • White-glove ops support — their SLA is passed through to their customers
Pain Points
  • Hyperscaler spot pricing eroding margin on fixed-price customer deals
  • Capacity crunches forcing them to turn away or delay customers
  • Vendor lock-in limiting their own pricing power downstream
  • Ops burden of managing multiple hyperscaler relationships
Buying Signals
  • Asking for blended rate cards across GPU SKUs — comparing suppliers
  • Questions about minimum cluster size and term flexibility
  • Customer growth story — "we're landing deals we can't fulfill"
  • Existing relationship with 1–2 other hardware vendors (competitive)
Common Objections & Responses
"We already have a CoreWeave/Lambda deal."
We're not asking you to replace them — diversification is a margin strategy. What's your blended cost today? Let's see if we can improve it on incremental capacity.
"We need flexibility to scale down."
We offer partial-cluster commitments with defined ramp schedules. Walk us through your demand forecast and we'll build a structure around it.
"Your minimums are too high."
What's your current utilization rate? If you're near capacity, the math usually works. We can start at 64 GPUs and structure growth options into the contract.
30-Second Pitch
"Your margin is determined before your first customer signs — it's locked in by what you pay for hardware. Nodestream gives you a fixed, predictable cost basis on dedicated bare-metal capacity with volume pricing that improves as you grow. You stop losing deals to capacity crunches and stop watching your margin compress every time spot prices spike."
Typical ACV$15M – $60M
Deal Length24 – 48 months
Sales Cycle6 – 16 weeks
Key ToolsDeal Configurator (multi-tier pricing) → Full Quote with volume schedule
Persona 03 — Enterprise / Sovereign

The Enterprise & Sovereign

"We can't put sensitive workloads on public cloud — we need dedicated, auditable, on-soil infrastructure."

Who They Are
  • Large enterprises, defense contractors, and government agencies
  • Organizations with strict data-sovereignty or air-gap requirements
  • Teams of 500–50,000+ employees; IT/procurement-led buying
  • Decision makers: CISO, CTO, Procurement, sometimes C-suite sponsor
What They Need
  • Data-residency guarantees — specific jurisdiction, no cross-border transfer
  • Physical isolation — dedicated racks, no logical multi-tenancy
  • Audit trails, compliance documentation (FedRAMP, ITAR, ISO 27001)
  • Long-term contracts with SLAs and financial penalties for breach
  • Procurement-friendly structure (MSA, SOW, PO-based billing)
Pain Points
  • Public cloud disqualified by legal, compliance, or national security policy
  • Internal HPC buildouts too slow and capital-intensive
  • Existing vendors can't provide the audit documentation security requires
  • Long procurement cycles — stakeholders span IT, legal, finance, security
Buying Signals
  • Mentioning FedRAMP, ITAR, CMMC, or data-residency in the first call
  • Asking about physical security, SOC 2, or third-party audits
  • IT/procurement involved from day one — not just technical buyer
  • Long decision timeline expectations (>90 days) mentioned proactively
Common Objections & Responses
"Our procurement cycle is 6 months minimum."
We work with that — we'll get a Letter of Intent in place, then your team drives the formal process. We can hold capacity with a soft reservation while you proceed.
"Can you pass our security review?"
We provide SOC 2 Type II, physical security documentation, and dedicated compliance support. Loop in your CISO and we'll set up a technical review call this week.
"We need on-prem — not a data center."
We offer isolated, geo-specific deployments that satisfy most sovereignty requirements without the capital burden of a full build. Let's review your compliance spec together.
30-Second Pitch
"Public cloud is off the table for your sensitive workloads — but building your own HPC environment takes 18 months and nine figures of capex. Nodestream gives you dedicated, physically isolated GPU infrastructure in your required jurisdiction, with the audit documentation your security team needs, on a contract structure your procurement team can actually process."
Typical ACV$5M – $25M
Deal Length36 – 60 months
Sales Cycle12 – 26 weeks
